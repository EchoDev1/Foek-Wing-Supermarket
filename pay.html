How to Pay

We strive to make your shopping experience as convenient as possible. We accept a variety of payment methods to suit your needs.

💶

Cash

We accept Euro (€) cash payments at all registers.

💳

Bank Cards

Bancontact, Maestro, Visa, and Mastercard are accepted. Contactless payments are fully supported.

📱

Mobile Payments

We accept Payconiq, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Alipay, and WeChat Pay for quick and easy mobile transactions.

💬

WeChat Pay

Seamlessly pay using your WeChat app. Just scan our QR code directly at the checkout counter.
